# Related Research

Some of the experiment structure and procedure is based on the paper [Spectral and Temporal Cues](https://kbdk-aub.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/fulldisplay?context=PC&vid=45KBDK_AUB:AUB&search_scope=MyInst_and_CI&tab=Everything&lang=da&docid=cdi_scitation_primary_10_1121_1_4955181) by Hjortkjær and McAdams that describes two similar experiments in which subjects were asked to give similarity ratings between sounds made by impacting different materials.

In their second experiment, where they removed the spectral content of the sounds to only retain the envelope they describe:

>  Listeners showed no sensitivity between any of the material categories without spectral cues, suggesting that they cannot use envelope cues for material discrimination independently of frequency information.

Indicating that the spectral content of a sound is essential to discriminate between characteristics of different sounds, which relates to this project which will focus on timbre perception.
